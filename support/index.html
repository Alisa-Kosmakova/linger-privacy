Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Linger works with no sign-up — just open it and start.

How does it work?
Look at a painting, guess it, reveal it, and rate how well you knew it. Linger uses spaced repetition to bring each painting back just before you'd forget it. Selected works also open into a short story about what's happening in the picture.

Is my progress saved?
Yes — your learning progress is stored locally on your device.

Report a problem with a story
You can flag any story from inside the app, or email us at the address above.
