Privacy Policy for Linger

Last updated: 14 June 2026

Linger (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is an art-learning app that helps you understand and remember great paintings. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your choices. We’ve kept it short because the app collects very little.

No account, no sign-in

Linger does not require an account. We never ask for your name, email address, password, or any login. There is no user profile.

What we collect

1. Anonymous usage analytics (via Pendo)

We use Pendo to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Pendo collects anonymous usage data: which screens you view, taps and interactions, and a few in-app events (for example, grading how well you knew a painting, or opening and finishing a story). It also collects basic device information (device model, operating-system version, language) and an anonymous, app-generated identifier. This information is not linked to your real-world identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

2. Issue reports (via Supabase)

If you choose to report a problem with a story, we send the following to our database: the painting/story the report is about, the slide you were viewing, your optional written note, the app version, and your platform (iOS or Android). We only receive this if you actively submit a report. Please do not include personal information in the optional note.

3. Stored only on your device

Your learning progress (how well you knew each painting) is stored locally on your device to schedule future reviews. It is not sent to us and is removed if you delete the app.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Third-party services

The services above process limited data on our behalf. Their privacy policies:

  • Pendo — pendo.io/legal/privacy-policy
  • Supabase — supabase.com/privacy

Data retention

Anonymous analytics are retained only as long as needed to improve the app. Issue reports are kept long enough to investigate and fix the reported problem.

Children’s privacy

Linger is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains some classical artworks depicting nudity and historical violence, reflected in its age rating.

Your rights

Because the data we collect is anonymous and not tied to your identity, we generally cannot connect it to you individually. If you have any questions or requests regarding your data (including rights under the GDPR or similar laws), contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
